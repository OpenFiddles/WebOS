Acceptable Use Policy

This policy describes baseline expectations for using WebOS and any OpenFiddles service that links to this page.

Adoption: OpenFiddles approves and adopts this Acceptable Use Policy for WebOS as of November 20, 2026. Use of WebOS must comply with this policy, the applicable UTAC, product-specific terms, and applicable law.

1. Use the service lawfully

You may use WebOS only for lawful purposes and in ways that respect the rights, safety, privacy, and security of others. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account or workspace, subject to applicable law and the product’s account-security features.

2. Prohibited activity

You must not use WebOS or its connected services to:

  • Break applicable laws, facilitate fraud, impersonation, theft, harassment, or other unlawful conduct.
  • Access accounts, systems, networks, files, or data without authorization.
  • Probe, scan, exploit, disrupt, overload, or interfere with a service or another person’s device or network without explicit permission.
  • Upload or distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, credential-stealing tools, or other harmful code.
  • Send spam, deceptive messages, phishing attempts, or unsolicited bulk communications.
  • Infringe intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, or other rights, or distribute content you do not have permission to share.
  • Evade access controls, usage limits, payment controls, moderation, or security protections.
  • Misuse hardware permissions, browser APIs, storage, networking, virtual machines, or compute resources in a way that harms others or violates the service’s stated limits.
  • Interfere with another user’s access, data, workspace, or normal use of the service.

3. Accounts, content, and permissions

Keep account credentials private and use only accounts you are authorized to access. Do not misrepresent your identity or authority. You are responsible for ensuring that content you upload, create, share, or distribute is lawful and that you have the necessary rights and permissions.

Grant browser and device permissions only when needed. WebOS features that request access to USB, serial, HID, Bluetooth, camera, microphone, files, or other device capabilities remain subject to browser permission prompts and device support. Do not attempt to bypass those protections.

4. Security research and testing

Do not test or attempt to exploit systems you do not own or have explicit permission to assess. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through the project’s published security contact or GitLab’s vulnerability reporting flow. Avoid accessing, changing, or disclosing other users’ data during research.

5. Shared, school, and organization workspaces

Users of shared workspaces must follow the workspace owner’s reasonable rules and respect role-based access. Administrators should communicate their policies to members and should not use administrative access for unrelated or unauthorized monitoring. School and youth-oriented deployments should apply appropriate safeguards and follow applicable student-privacy and child-protection requirements.

6. Enforcement

Where reasonably necessary and permitted by law, OpenFiddles may investigate credible reports, restrict abusive activity, suspend or terminate access, remove prohibited content, or preserve relevant records for security and legal purposes. Any action should be proportionate to the risk and consistent with applicable law and the published privacy notice.

Where appropriate and permitted by law, OpenFiddles will provide notice of significant restrictions and may offer a way to request review. Urgent action may be taken first when reasonably necessary to protect users, systems, or legal interests. No particular response time or outcome is guaranteed.

7. Reporting concerns

Report abuse, security concerns, or suspected policy violations through the official project issue or security-reporting channels. Do not post credentials, private personal information, exploit details that could put others at risk, or another person’s private content in a public report.

8. Updates

This AUP may be revised when WebOS features, service limits, or legal requirements change. The version published at this URL is the version users should consult, subject to any effective-date and notice requirements under applicable law.

This approved policy should be read together with the WebOS UTAC and the applicable privacy notice. It does not itself authorize undisclosed personal-data collection or override rights and protections under applicable law.
