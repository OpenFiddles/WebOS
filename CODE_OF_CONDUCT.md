# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

WebOS contributors and community members should be able to participate in a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than personal attributes.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Help keep discussions relevant, accessible, and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and the publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and community interactions when someone is representing or participating in the WebOS project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the GitLab project. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in public issues. Maintainers should review reports promptly, fairly, and confidentially where possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or take other actions appropriate to the circumstances. The goal is to protect a respectful project community.
